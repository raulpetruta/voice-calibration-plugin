# Voice Profile Template

Use this template to generate the user's voice profile. Fill every section with specific, actionable observations — not vague generalities. Each section should contain enough detail that someone could imitate this writing style from the profile alone.

---

```markdown
# Voice Profile: {name}

## Summary

{2-3 sentences capturing the essence of this person's writing. What makes it recognizably theirs? Think of this as the "elevator pitch" for their style.}

## Sentence Structure

{How do they build sentences? Length patterns, complexity, fragments, typical openers. Include specific examples from their samples.}

## Vocabulary & Word Choice

{Register level, characteristic words/phrases, specificity vs. abstraction, hedging vs. assertion. Note any slang, profanity patterns, or domain-specific language.}

## Tone & Personality

{Humor style, warmth, directness, energy level, self-awareness. How do they relate to the reader? What's the emotional temperature of their writing?}

## Punctuation & Formatting Habits

{Distinctive punctuation patterns — em dashes, ellipses, exclamation marks, parentheticals. Paragraph length, use of lists, capitalization quirks, emoji usage.}

## Conversational Style

{How do they sound in casual vs. more considered writing? Filler words, discourse markers, rhetorical questions, asides. How do they transition between topics?}

## Narrative Approach

{How do they tell stories or explain things? Detail level, pacing, dialogue usage, perspective. Do they build tension or present things flat?}

## Sample Phrases

{5-10 exact phrases, constructions, or sentence patterns extracted from their writing samples. These are the building blocks for imitating their voice.}

- "{phrase 1}"
- "{phrase 2}"
- "{phrase 3}"
- "{phrase 4}"
- "{phrase 5}"

## What to Avoid

{5-8 specific things that would sound wrong in this person's voice. These are the most important guardrails. Be concrete.}

- Do not {specific anti-pattern 1}
- Do not {specific anti-pattern 2}
- Do not {specific anti-pattern 3}
- Do not {specific anti-pattern 4}
- Do not {specific anti-pattern 5}
```

---

## Quality Checklist

Before presenting the profile to the user, verify:

- [ ] Every section contains **specific observations**, not generic descriptions
- [ ] At least 3 sections include **direct quotes** from writing samples
- [ ] The **Sample Phrases** section has real extracts, not fabricated examples
- [ ] The **What to Avoid** section is concrete enough to actually prevent mistakes
- [ ] Reading the profile, you could distinguish this person's writing from someone else's
